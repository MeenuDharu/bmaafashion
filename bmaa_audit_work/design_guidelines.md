# Bmaa Fashion Ecommerce Design Guidelines

## Design Approach

**Reference-Based Approach**: Inspired by sustainable agriculture leaders like Tower Garden and AeroGarden, focusing on clean, nature-centric ecommerce layouts that emphasize growth and sustainability.

## Core Design Elements

### A. Color Palette

**Primary Colors:**

- Primary: #2E8B57 (sea green) - main brand color for headers, CTAs
- Secondary: #228B22 (forest green) - navigation, accents
- Accent: #90EE90 (light green) - highlights, success states
- Background: #F0FFF0 (honeydew) - page backgrounds
- Text: #2F4F2F (dark olive green) - body text
- White: #FFFFFF - cards, contrast areas

### B. Typography

- **Primary Font**: Poppins (headers, navigation, CTAs)
- **Secondary Font**: Open Sans (body text, descriptions)
- **Hierarchy**: Bold headers (24-32px), medium subheaders (18-20px), regular body (16px)

### C. Layout System

**Tailwind Spacing**: Use units of 4, 6, 8, 12, 16 for consistent spacing

- Padding/margins: p-4, p-6, p-8
- Gaps: gap-6, gap-8
- Heights: h-12, h-16 for buttons/inputs

### D. Component Library

**Product Cards**: Rounded corners (12px), hover elevation, image-first layout
**Navigation**: Clean horizontal nav with green accent on active states
**Buttons**: Primary (sea green), secondary (outline), rounded-lg
**Forms**: Clean inputs with forest green focus states
**Shopping Cart**: Floating cart icon with item count badge

### E. Key Design Patterns

- **Card-based layouts** for product displays
- **Generous whitespace** for breathing room
- **Nature-inspired imagery** throughout
- **Mobile-first responsive design**
- **Organic rounded corners** (12px radius consistently)

## Images

**Hero Section**: Large hero image showcasing lush hydroponic gardens or growing systems (full viewport height)
**Product Images**: High-quality photos of hydroponic kits, growing systems, and fresh produce
**Category Banners**: Smaller banner images for different product categories
**About Section**: Images of sustainable farming practices and BMAA FASHION facilities
**Logo**: Use provided BMAA FASHION logo (black/white background variants)

## Specific Layout Sections

1. **Header**: Logo, navigation, cart icon
2. **Hero**: Full-width image with value proposition overlay
3. **Featured Products**: 3-4 bestselling kits in card grid
4. **Product Categories**: Visual category navigation
5. **About Preview**: Brief company story with imagery
6. **Footer**: Contact info, social links, policies

**Payment Integration**: Razorpay integration for secure checkout processing in Indian market
